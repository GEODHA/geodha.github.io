Until now our articles lived in an embedded Notion page. It worked, but it hid our writing behind someone else's interface and made it hard for anyone outside the core team to contribute.

So we moved the blog **into the website itself**.

## What changed

- **Posts are plain Markdown files** in the [`content/blog`](https://github.com/GEODHA/geodha.github.io/tree/main/content/blog) folder of our public repository. Every edit is versioned and reviewable.
- **Anyone can contribute.** Volunteers with access can write in our editor at [/cms](/cms/) using their GitHub account; everyone else can open a pull request or simply email a pitch to [contact@geodha.org](mailto:contact@geodha.org?subject=Blog%20pitch).
- **Drafts go through review.** Each new post becomes a pull request, so a second pair of eyes checks facts and data before anything goes live.

## What we'd love to publish

- Ward stories — what's changed (or hasn't) on your street, with photos
- Analyses of open civic data on waste, water, air or roads
- How-tos on segregation, composting, bulk-waste generator rules and escalation
- Lessons from cleanup drives, RWAs and community organisations

> GEODHA is a volunteer-run community project. The more people write, map and build with us, the more useful it becomes for every ward in the city.

See you in the comments of our next pull request. 👋
