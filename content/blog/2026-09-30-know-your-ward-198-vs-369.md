In 2025 the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) split the city into **five corporations** — Central, East, North, South and West — and redrew the map into **369 wards**. Most civic datasets, complaint histories and helplines, though, still use the **198 BBMP wards** from 2010.

That makes a simple question — *"which ward am I in, and who do I call?"* — surprisingly hard.

## The Neighbourhood Scorecard

Our [dashboard](/dashboard) now has a **Neighbourhood Scorecard**. You can:

1. Search by your **old BBMP ward name** (198 list),
2. Search by your **new GBA ward name** (369 list), or
3. Type any **place, landmark or address** — or paste a Google Maps link — and we'll work out the ward for you.

The scorecard then shows both ward identities side by side, which **corporation** you fall under, the issues reported in the area, recommended actions and the **key contacts** to escalate to.

## Why both numbers?

Complaint data and ward-marshal phone numbers are still organised by the 198 wards. New elected representatives and corporation offices follow the 369-ward map. Until everything migrates, showing both is the most useful thing we can do.

## Help us keep it accurate

Ward boundaries come from the GBA's notified delimitation (December 2025), published as open data by [OpenCity](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/gba-wards-delimitation-2025). If you spot a mismatch or an outdated contact, [tell us](mailto:contact@geodha.org?subject=Scorecard%20correction) — or fix it yourself on [GitHub](https://github.com/GEODHA/geodha.github.io).
